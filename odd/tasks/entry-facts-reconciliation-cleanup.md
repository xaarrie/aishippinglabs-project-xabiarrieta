## Backend contract and acceptance criteria
- Extraction persists immutable `Generated` proposals/additions keyed by extraction job ID, completed-job hash/fingerprint/revision/lease evidence; no effective facts or candidate embeddings. Historical effective extraction evidence is preserved.
- Raw Generated drafts cannot be accepted as reconciled proposals. Existing planner consumes server-stored candidates and produces `Pending` reconciliation proposals; acceptance is bodyless, atomic and idempotent for the SAME accepted proposal.
- Pre-acceptance edits/regeneration mark old proposals/jobs `Superseded` and revoke leases. Restoring old source text must not revive old work. A stale worker cannot commit over new source.
- Accepted locks must hold at application, service AND DbContext boundaries, including zero-addition accepted evidence. New proposals/second acceptance on the same accepted source are forbidden; unrelated new entries remain valid.
- Source moves reserve BOTH original and new timeline CAS baselines BEFORE lock checks; source columns alone cannot detect acceptance interleavings.
- `POST /api/entries/{id}/fact-generation` with `{}` starts/continues generation after save independently of hosted poller; regenerate with `{regenerate:true,expectedGenerationId:<id>}`. Saving the entry itself must not depend on provider availability.
- GET facts/generation response exposes `lifecycleState`, `generationId`, `canEdit`, `canGenerate`, `canRegenerate`, jobs/backoff/errors. States: NotGenerated, Loading, Retry, Failed, Empty, Pending, Reconciled.
- Candidates are `proposals[].additions` from Generated proposal with `proposalId == generationId`, NOT a root candidates property. Reconciliation/answer/accept routes remain separate.
- CLI Saved means persisted NONEMPTY pending evidence, NOT activation; Empty requires verified zero-addition Generated evidence; Stale means mismatched/superseded source/generation. Neither runner accepts or embeds. Retry selects unique current generation despite Superseded history.
